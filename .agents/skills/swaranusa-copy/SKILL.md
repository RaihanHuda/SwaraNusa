name: swaranusa-copy
description: Write and review Indonesian interface copy for SwaraNusa. Use when adding page text, calls to action, empty states, request status messages or cultural articles in this project.
---

Write for a person choosing a province or following a concrete learning task. Use familiar Indonesian. Name the action on buttons: Pilih provinsi, Baca pengenalan, Kirim request, Cari pendamping lain. Match the factual availability of content. State whether an account or profile is a demo.

Apply stop-slop: cut filler and grand claims; use active voice, concrete places and varied sentence lengths. Avoid em dashes, binary slogans, forced triples, 'jelajahi kekayaan tak ternilai', 'di era digital', 'lebih dari sekadar', 'perjalanan tak terlupakan', and decorative statistics. Keep visitor-facing text free of development jargon.

Keep sources with cultural claims. Identify a community and variations when known. Distinguish introductory reading from a practice guide. Don't fabricate expert partnerships, videos, prices, visitor numbers or verification. Say what a visitor can do when content or experts are missing.

Review: score directness, rhythm, trust, authenticity, density from 1 to 10. Revise if total below 35. Check three cases: province introduction, no expert available, and successfully submitted request. Save reviews in docs/copy-review.md. The website itself is the human-review surface; no separate skill benchmark is part of this build.
